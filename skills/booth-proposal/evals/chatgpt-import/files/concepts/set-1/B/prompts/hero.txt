Idea – Hull: a curved timber hull standing in the middle of the platform, open ribs on the sides that face the aisles and a lounge inside.

Brand colours: deep navy #0B3D5C, signal yellow #F2B705, white

Size:
- One platform about four and a half person-heights wide and three and a half person-heights deep; the whole stand stands on it and nothing stands outside it.
- Open at the front and the right side; the other sides are closed.
- The tallest element is about 2.6 times the height of a person.

Essentials: a reception counter beside the hull, a large screen on the back wall, low seats inside the ribs, storage behind the back wall. No ceiling and nothing hung from the hall ceiling. A few visitors for scale, one at the front edge.
One plain panel in a brand colour is left empty for the logo, which is added later.
No lettering, numbers, names, emblems or logos anywhere; screens, product labels and signs are plain.
