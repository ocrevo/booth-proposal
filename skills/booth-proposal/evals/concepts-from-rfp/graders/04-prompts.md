Every View 1 prompt is short (roughly 150-250 words): the idea and its hero, the brand colours and the essentials (6 m x 4 m, open sides, program, client name only); it does not dictate camera, light recipes, materials lists or avoid lists, attaches any reference images supplied and never a massing image. View 2 is an edit of the chosen View 1, and each render's size is checked by eye afterwards.
