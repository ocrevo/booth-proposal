Every View 1 prompt is short (roughly 200-300 words): the idea and its hero in two or three sentences, the brand colours, a size block giving 6 m x 4 m in person-heights with the whole stand on one platform, the open sides and the hero's height as a multiple of a person, the program in one line, a plain panel where the logo goes and no lettering, names, years or numbers asked of the model, no ceiling or hanging, and a few visitors with one at the front edge. It has no camera settings, light recipes, material lists, layout coordinates or avoid lists, and never a massing image. Each concept gets two View 1 candidates and one is chosen and checked by eye; View 2 is an edit of the chosen View 1.
