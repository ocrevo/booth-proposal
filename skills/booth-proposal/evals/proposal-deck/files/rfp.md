# Request for Proposal – Exhibition Stand, LabWorld Europe Summit 2027

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE. Halvard Instruments, LabWorld Europe Summit and every fact below are invented for testing the booth-proposal skill.

## 1. About the client

Halvard Instruments builds precision instruments for life-science laboratories: balances, pipetting systems and automated liquid handlers.

- Founded in 1952 as a two-person workshop making laboratory balances.
- 1981: introduced the first fully automated pipetting robot in Europe.
- Instruments in daily use in more than 40 countries.
- Every balance is calibrated by hand before it leaves the factory.
- Brand line: "Precision you can trust."
- Brand colours: Halvard Green (#0F4D3A) and white. Logo file attached: halvard-logo.svg.

## 2. Event

- LabWorld Europe Summit 2027, Hall 3, exhibition centre in a European city.
- Conference: 9–11 March 2027. Exhibition: 10–11 March 2027.
- Build-up: 7–8 March 2027. Dismantle: 11 March from 18:00.
- Visitors: laboratory managers, scientists, procurement teams, distributors.

## 3. Stand

- Size: 6 m × 4 m (24 m²), corner stand. The organiser has not yet confirmed which two sides are open.
- Maximum height: 4.5 m. Hanging structures are not permitted in Hall 3.
- Power: 10 kW available. All materials must carry a B1 fire certificate.

## 4. Requirements

1. Theme: 75 years of Halvard precision.
2. Display three flagship instruments: the Halvard A75 balance, the PX-8 pipetting system and the LH-400 liquid handler.
3. At least one 75-inch screen for product films.
4. An interactive station where visitors can test their pipetting accuracy.
5. A seated meeting area for four people.
6. A coffee bar serving visitors throughout the show.
7. A branded giveaway visitors can take home.
8. A lockable storage room of at least 1.5 m².
9. Four staff on the stand at all times.

## 5. Deliverables

1. Stand design concept with renderings and a floor plan.
2. Graphic designs for two wall panels.
3. Build schedule.
4. Itemised price.
