Each View 1 prompt has one short line on the attached image: what to keep and evolve (the arch and the green; for a concept that starts elsewhere, the palette and the glow only) and what not to copy. The prompt does not describe the old stand at length in place of the image, does not turn it into a list of things to avoid, and does not ask the model to paint the logo, the name or any text: it asks for a plain logo panel.
